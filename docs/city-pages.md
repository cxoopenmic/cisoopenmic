# City and event URLs

City pages are permanent hubs:

- `/ca/calgary/`
- `/ca/montreal/`
- `/ca/ottawa/`
- `/ca/toronto/`
- `/us/boston/`

Montreal is bilingual. English is the default city hub and French has its own shareable route:

- `/ca/montreal/`
- `/ca/montreal/fr/`

Individual event pages live below their city and use compact, lowercase slugs without hyphens. For example:

- `/ca/toronto/fall2026/`
- `/us/boston/fall2026/`

Home cards always link to the permanent city hub. When registration opens, the city hub should link to the event page, and the event page should hold the Luma registration link, date, venue and event-specific sponsor information.
