Meet your peers in Montreal.

CISO Open Mic brings CISOs and other security leaders together to share insights, experiences and challenges. A local MC and Event Director guide the evening at Club Saint-James de Montréal. Aspiring leaders and other community and industry stakeholders are also welcome to apply. No sales pitches.

What to expect

  • Meet fellow security leaders over drinks and hors d’oeuvres.
  • Take part in open mic conversations or attend to listen and learn.
  • Up to nine trophies will be awarded in recognition of contributions.

Who can attend

Participation is curated for CISOs, other security leaders, aspiring leaders and relevant community and industry stakeholders. Space is limited and registration requires host approval. Approved guests receive confirmation; other applicants may be waitlisted.

A registration request is not a confirmed place; wait for your approval email before attending.

How to take part

  • Request to join through the registration form. You can also propose a speaking topic.
  • Join us on November 5, 2026, from 5:00 to 9:00 p.m. at Club Saint-James de Montréal, 1145 Avenue Union, Montréal, QC H3B 3C2.
  • Share your perspective in a five-minute talk, or listen to your peers.
  • Connect with other attendees and continue the conversation.

Before attending, read how the night works and event rules and the guidelines for taking the mic.

Interested in helping shape CISO Open Mic Montreal as a Judge, Master of Ceremonies (MC), or Event Director? Apply to join the Montreal team.

Confidentiality

Registration requires agreement to the Chatham House Rule: you may use information shared during the event, but must not reveal the identity or affiliation of a speaker or other participant, or attribute their remarks.

Photography and video

Photos and video may be captured for event documentation and promotion. Attendance indicates consent to appear in these materials. If you prefer not to be photographed or recorded, tell event staff when you arrive so they can accommodate you.
