Meet your peers in Ottawa.

A conversation shaped by the people in the room

CISO Open Mic is a platform for CISOs, security leaders, and other stakeholders to share insights, experiences and challenges. Participation is curated to support meaningful conversations.

A local Master of Ceremonies and Event Director guide the evening. The focus is on the community—not on promoting the people or organizations behind the event.

Take the mic or take it all in

Bring an idea, a lesson learned, a challenge or an experience worth sharing. Each speaker has five minutes. No slide deck is required, and sales pitches and product demonstrations are not permitted.

You do not have to speak to contribute. Listening, asking thoughtful questions and connecting with others are all part of the experience.

Expect candid perspectives, useful discussions and opportunities to build relationships with people who understand the work.

Registration and approval

Submit your registration through Luma using your organization-issued business email address. Personal email addresses, such as Gmail or Hotmail, will be declined.

Every registration is reviewed. Submitting a request does not confirm admission—please wait for your approval through Luma before attending. Previous participation does not guarantee approval for another event.

Attendance is curated. A declined registration should not be resubmitted with the same information in the expectation of a different outcome.

There are no walk-ins, and you cannot bring an unregistered friend or colleague. Each person must register separately and receive approval. Sales representatives from non-sponsoring organizations are not eligible to attend, and there is no “try before you sponsor” option.

Interested in speaking?

Indicate your interest and proposed topic during registration. Topics should be relevant to the security leadership community and grounded in useful experience, insight or a genuine challenge.

Registration does not guarantee a place at the mic. Speaking opportunities are selected separately.

If you commit to speaking, arrive prepared and ready when called. If you indicate that you might speak, you should still be prepared. Keep within your five minutes, respect other perspectives and make room for others—no grandstanding.

Promoting another event or cause requires explicit approval from the CISO Open Mic team.

A confirmed speaker who fails to attend without cancelling through Luma will be banned from future participation. If you cannot attend, decline through Luma; telling an organizer or another participant does not update your registration.

Before you arrive

Please review the club’s dress code. Anyone who does not meet it upon arrival will not be admitted.

Food and alcohol should be enjoyed in moderation. We reserve the right to stop service to anyone.

Any activities offered before or after the main event have their own admission arrangements. Approval for CISO Open Mic does not automatically include access to those activities.

Confidentiality and respect

The event follows the Chatham House Rule: you may use information shared during the discussion, but you must not reveal the identity or affiliation of a speaker or any other participant, or attribute their remarks.

Respect others’ privacy. Do not share confidential, proprietary or identifying information that you are not authorized to disclose.

Recognition

Awards are determined solely at the judges’ discretion. The MC, Event Director and CISO Open Mic representatives do not influence those decisions.

You do not need to win an award—or take the mic—to make a meaningful contribution to the evening.

Photography and sharing

Photos and video may be captured for event documentation and promotion. If you prefer not to be photographed or recorded, please tell the event team when you arrive so they can help accommodate your preference.

LinkedIn posts and other positive coverage are appreciated, but never expected. Please respect the Chatham House Rule when sharing. Use #CISOOpenMic to help us find your post; we may not see or respond to every mention.
