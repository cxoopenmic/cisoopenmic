Meet your peers in Toronto.

An evening for CISOs and senior security leaders to exchange ideas openly at Toronto’s National Club. Expect peer-led conversations without sales pitches, moderators or slide presentations.

What to expect

  • Meet fellow security leaders over drinks and hors d’oeuvres.
  • Take part in open mic conversations or attend to listen and learn.
  • Up to nine trophies will be awarded in recognition of contributions.

Who can attend

Attendance is for senior security leaders at established organizations. Space is limited. Register through the form below using your organization-issued business email.

How to take part

  • Register to attend. You can also propose a speaking topic.
  • Join us on November 24, 2026, from 5:00 to 9:00 p.m. at The National Club, 303 Bay Street, Toronto.
  • Share your perspective in a five-minute talk, or listen to your peers. Mic spots are selected separately from event registration.
  • Connect with other attendees and continue the conversation.

Before attending, read how the night works and event rules and the guidelines for taking the mic.

Interested in helping shape CISO Open Mic Toronto as a Judge, Master of Ceremonies (MC), or Event Director? Apply to join the Toronto team.

Confidentiality

The event follows the Chatham House Rule: you may use information shared during the event, but must not reveal the identity or affiliation of a speaker or other participant, or attribute their remarks.

Photography and video

Photos and video may be captured for event documentation and promotion. Attendance indicates consent to appear in these materials. If you prefer not to be photographed or recorded, tell event staff when you arrive so they can accommodate you.
