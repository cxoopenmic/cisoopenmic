Whether this is your first time at the mic or you want to sharpen a story you already tell, bring an idea, a lesson learned, or a challenge from your experience.

  1. 01

    Find the point.

    Choose the one idea you want people to remember. Focus on something useful to fellow security leaders.

  2. 02

    Shape the story.

    Work on an opening, a clear thread, and a takeaway that fit your five minutes.

  3. 03

    Practise out loud.

    Practise your pace and delivery, and use constructive feedback to make the message your own.

Come as you are

An idea is enough
to get started.

You do not need a finished speech or a slide deck. Bring a topic you care about and a willingness to practise. Keep it grounded in your experience, free of sales pitches, and respectful of information you cannot share.

How to sign up

  1. Open the Toronto event registration form.
  2. Find the Mic Ready question and choose “Yes — sign me up.”
  3. Complete and submit your event registration. The team will follow up with Mic Ready session details.

Session timing and arrangements will be shared separately. Event admission and speaking opportunities remain subject to the usual approval and selection process.

A few things to know.

Do I need speaking experience?

No. Mic Ready is for people preparing to take the mic, including first-time speakers and those who want to refine their delivery.

Does signing up guarantee a speaking slot or an award?

No. Mic Ready supports your preparation. Speaking opportunities are selected separately, and awards remain at the judges’ discretion.

When and where is the coaching session?

Session details will be shared separately with people who sign up. The Toronto event’s listed time and venue refer to the main event.

What if I have already registered?

Contact the team using the Toronto event inquiry form and ask to join Mic Ready. There is no need to submit a second event registration.
