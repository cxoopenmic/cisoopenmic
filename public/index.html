About CISO Open Mic

Take the mic—or take it all in.

CISO Open Mic is an interactive event where CISOs and senior security leaders can take the mic for a five-minute talk on a topic that matters to them—or listen, learn, and connect with peers. A local MC guides the evening, and a panel of four judges recognizes the strongest contributions. There are no vendor pitches or slide decks; the focus is on original perspectives, practical insight, and community recognition.
